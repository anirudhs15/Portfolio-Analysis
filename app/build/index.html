The rating and price target published by brokerage analysts, with how many and when. Their opinion, not a recommendation from this app.

The lowest and highest price in the past year, with today's price marked.
Beta
How much the portfolio tends to move when the Nifty moves 1%.
Volatility
The typical size of yearly ups and downs.
Fund of funds
A fund that invests in other funds or ETFs, such as a silver FoF that holds silver ETFs.
