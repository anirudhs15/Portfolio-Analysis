        Data notes
          What the terms mean
          Effective holdings
          How many equal-sized holdings your portfolio behaves like. 24 holdings that act like 13 means a few large positions dominate.
          Driver
          Whether a move came from the market, a market segment (small or mid caps), a commodity price, or news about the company itself.
          52-week range
          The lowest and highest price in the past year, with today's price marked.
          Beta
          How much the portfolio tends to move when the Nifty moves 1%.
          Volatility
          The typical size of yearly ups and downs, from daily price changes.
          Fund of funds (FoF)
          A mutual fund that invests in other funds or ETFs, such as a silver FoF that holds silver ETFs.
